# HowToResearch
A guide to the the world of literature

Research may sound very overwhelming and humongous and serious topic to an amateur like me.
So bear with me while I figure out "HowToResearch".

Based on an article I read about understanding research papers:
- First we need to go through the abstract and introduction to get an overview of the topic and uderstand what question that particular paper is trying to answer.
- Then we try to (or atleast pretend to) understand the main content. Get an idea with whatever familiar words you come across and see if you can find answers for these questions: “What question is the author trying to answer?”; “Is the question framed appropriately based on the gaps identified by the author in the existing literature?”; “Which methods were used to answer the question?”; “Are the methods appropriate?”; “What are the key results?”; “If any statistical tests are used, are they appropriate and robust?”; “Do the data and results support the conclusions drawn?”; and “What are the limitations of the study?
- Now we need to make a short summary of what we understand and a list of all that we do not understand.
-Next step can be to try to understand the topics which seem relevant from this list.
